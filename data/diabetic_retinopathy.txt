Diabetic retinopathy is a diabetes-related eye disease
that affects blood vessels in the retina.

Symptoms:
- Blurred or fluctuating vision
- Dark spots or floaters
- Difficulty seeing at night
- Vision loss in advanced stages

Causes:
- Long-term uncontrolled diabetes
- High blood pressure
- High cholesterol

Treatment:
Treatment includes blood sugar control, laser therapy,
anti-VEGF injections, and surgery in advanced cases.

Medical advice:
Regular eye exams are essential for people with diabetes.
