A healthy eye has clear vision and normal eye pressure.
The retina appears flat and intact.
The optic disc has sharp and well-defined margins.
Blood vessels have normal caliber and distribution.
There is no hemorrhage or exudate.
The macula appears normal without swelling.
Visual acuity is clear at distance and near.
Regular eye checkups help maintain eye health.
Good lighting and screen breaks reduce eye strain.
Healthy lifestyle supports long-term vision.
