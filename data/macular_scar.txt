Macular star refers to a star-shaped pattern of hard exudates in the macula.
It is often associated with neuroretinitis.
Patients may experience sudden vision loss.
The pattern results from lipid deposition.
Macular star is commonly seen in optic disc inflammation.
Infections can be an underlying cause.
Fundus examination reveals radial exudates.
Vision usually improves with treatment.
Steroids or antibiotics may be required.
Early diagnosis improves outcomes.
