Glaucoma is a group of eye conditions that damage the optic nerve,
often due to increased pressure inside the eye.

Symptoms may include:
- Gradual loss of peripheral vision
- Tunnel vision in advanced stages
- Eye pain and redness (acute cases)
- Headache and nausea

Risk factors:
- Family history
- Diabetes
- High eye pressure
- Age over 40

Treatment:
Glaucoma is managed with eye drops, oral medications, laser treatment,
or surgery to lower eye pressure.

Emergency warning:
Sudden eye pain with vision loss may indicate acute glaucoma
and requires immediate medical care.
