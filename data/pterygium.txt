Pterygium is a benign growth on the conjunctiva.
It extends onto the cornea.
Sun exposure is a major risk factor.
It is more common in tropical climates.
Patients may experience irritation or redness.
Vision is affected if it covers the pupil.
Early stages require no treatment.
Lubricating eye drops relieve symptoms.
Surgery is recommended for advanced cases.
Recurrence may occur after removal.
