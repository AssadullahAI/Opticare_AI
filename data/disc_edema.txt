Disc edema refers to swelling of the optic disc.
It is often caused by increased intracranial pressure.
Vision may initially remain normal.
Patients may experience headache or nausea.
Bilateral disc edema is common.
Papilledema is a specific type of disc edema.
Fundoscopic examination shows blurred disc margins.
Disc edema is a medical emergency.
Neuroimaging is required for diagnosis.
Treatment depends on the underlying cause.
