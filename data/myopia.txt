Myopia is also known as nearsightedness.
Distant objects appear blurry.
It occurs due to elongation of the eyeball.
Myopia usually begins in childhood.
High myopia increases the risk of retinal detachment.
Corrective lenses improve vision.
Refractive surgery may be an option.
Progression can be slowed with lifestyle changes.
Regular eye exams are important.
Severe myopia may lead to complications.
